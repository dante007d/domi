Pro Tips & Troubleshooting

1. The "Clean Slate" Reset

Before changing any blocks, click Reset. This clears any "locked" orientation and returns the pawn to North (0°).

2. Space to Turn

Ensure your character isn't squeezed against a wall when turning. Move into open space first to allow the rotation animation to trigger clearly.

3. Test One Block

Stuck? Drag all blocks to the Trash and test just one Turn Right block. If it rotates 90°, the engine is working perfectly!
